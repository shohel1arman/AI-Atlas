Time Series & Forecasting

Data that remembers its order

When time is an axis, order carries meaning: a trend drifts, a season repeats, noise wobbles on top. Pull a series apart into its components, smooth away the jitter, then project the next stretch with a confidence band that honestly widens as you look further ahead.

Build the series

Smoothing trades responsiveness for stability. A simple moving average averages the last W points equally; an exponential moving average weights recent points more, decaying by α. Widen the window and the line lags; raise α and it hugs the data.

raw (faint) · SMA (cyan) · EMA (amber)
SMAt = mean(xt−W+1…t)   EMAt = α·xt + (1−α)·EMAt−1

Smoothers

Low α ≈ long memory (smooth, laggy). High α ≈ short memory (reactive).

Baselines first

Naive (last value), seasonal-naive (last season), mean, and drift. Always beat these before reaching for ARIMA or deep nets.

ARIMA / ETS

ARIMA(p,d,q) models autocorrelation after differencing; SARIMA adds seasonality. ETS is the state-space form of exponential smoothing (Holt-Winters).

ML & deep

Gradient boosting on lag/rolling features, LSTMs, and Temporal Fusion Transformers — powerful, but validate with rolling-origin backtests to avoid leakage.
