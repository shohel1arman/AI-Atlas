What just happened

A model can’t read letters — it reads token IDs. A subword tokenizer keeps common words whole and splits rare ones into pieces (shown with ##). Each piece maps to a row in the embedding table.

Rule of thumb

~4 characters per token in English. That’s why context windows are counted in tokens, not words — and why your bill is too.

Term weights in this document

TF · IDF

Term frequency rewards words that appear often here. Inverse document frequency punishes words that appear everywhere. Multiply them and the distinctive words rise to the top.

Notice

Words like the, a, of score near zero — they’re in every document, so they carry no signal. This is how classic search ranks results.
